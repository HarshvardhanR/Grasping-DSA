Leetcode 242 : https://leetcode.com/problems/valid-anagram/description/

Trivial Approach : Two hashmap
Use two maps to store both string respectively and check if they are equal.

What if you are to ignore space and UpperCase letters? 
Just strip the string with space and put with a lower lettercase in two maps.

What if k-anagrams? You are allowed to change at most k elements
Use two frequency arrays and lastly check mismatch from first string. As anagram length should be same only checking
from one string gives us the imbalance as it is implied from definition of anagram.
