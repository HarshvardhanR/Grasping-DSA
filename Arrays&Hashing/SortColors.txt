Approach : TreeSet put and retrieve keyset uses extra space

Approach: Simply use any sorting technique

Better Approach - Just count zeros, ones and two and put them in an array (Two pass - One for counting, second for putting)

Optimal Approach: Using three pointers - low, mid, high
set low=0; mid =0; high = nums.length - 1
if mid=0 switch with low, increase both low and mid
mid = 1? mid ++;
mid = 2? switch with high, decrease high only
